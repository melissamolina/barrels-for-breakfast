THE HISTORY

It started in Playa Garza.

About 17 years ago, in a little cabin in front of the ocean in Playa Garza, Guanacaste, Costa Rica, the first Barrels for Breakfast surfboard bags were made by hand.

The problem was simple: the surfboard bags available felt generic. So discarded and leftover materials became the starting point — Costa Rican coffee sacks, old advertising banners, fabrics and even old towels for lining.

Surfers began bringing their boards to the cabin. Each board was measured. Materials were chosen. The construction changed depending on how the bag would actually be used — lighter for local riding and bikes, more protection for travel.

The bags made their way to surf shops in Playa Guiones, and sometimes people tried to buy them before they even reached the shop. Over the years, Barrels for Breakfast traveled beyond Costa Rica, including the Canary Islands, Ireland and Portugal.

Today the same idea continues: useful surf gear, made with intention, built around the surfer — now being prepared for its next production batch in Costa Rica.
